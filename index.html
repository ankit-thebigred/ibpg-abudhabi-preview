Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

"To create a progressive bilateral trade platform between India and the UAE, encouraging inclusive commercial partnerships that inspire entrepreneurship, networking, globalisation, innovation & job creation."

Our Vision
Our Mission
  • Serve as a facilitator linking businesses and governments
  • Promote investments and business developments in both UAE and India
  • Organise business and social events for networking and partnerships
  • Engage with Indian business chambers and councils globally
  • Create a platform to share knowledge and business opportunities
